When I step in

Plan gate

  • Read the Jira plan comment and the Given/When/Then scenarios.
  • Wrong or unclear: reply, loop until right.
  • Good: add ai-fix. It approves plan and scenarios.

MR gate

  • Needs ai-verified + ai-reviewed; read the verify and review notes on the MR. ai-review-findings = look at the review first.
  • Review, mark ready, add reviewers, merge.
  • Changes wanted: comment in Jira, mr pushes to the same MR.

Failure labels

  • triage-failed (analysis or reply failed): fix the cause, re-add triage.
  • ai-fix-failed: read the bot comment, fix plan, re-add ai-fix or take over with cf-work.
  • ai-verify-failed: read <KEY>.verify.md, fix the scenario, re-label, or take over.

Still not covered

Availability

  • The timer runs Mon–Fri 07–18 and only while I am logged in. A comment outside that window, or while the laptop sleeps, waits.
  • Nothing alerts when the dispatcher itself is dead; a missing “alive” heartbeat is the only sign.

Slots

  • Three long stages (impl, mr, a backend verify, an LLM conflict) can fill all three slots. reply is first in line but cannot preempt.

Visibility

  • Only my own MR notes trigger mr; reviewers’ MR notes wait until I comment.
  • A fix I push by hand leaves ai-verified standing; verify-fix pushes and pushes answering my comments are not re-reviewed.
  • Verify blocked (missing flag, no data) parks the ticket as ai-verify-failed before any review happened; triage-worker KEY --review runs it by hand.
  • triage-dash still judges “needs you” by the last comment, not the handled id.
